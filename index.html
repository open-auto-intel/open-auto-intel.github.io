What We Mean by Automated Intelligence

Not artificial intelligence that replaces humans. Automated intelligence that removes the grunt work so humans can focus on what matters. The philosophy isn't new — it's the same idea behind Puppet, Chef, Ansible, and Terraform. We just took it further.

Observe Everything

Metrics, logs, alerts, deployments, code changes, infrastructure state. Collect signals from every source. Correlate them automatically.

Learn Continuously

Every resolved incident teaches a new pattern. No retraining, no fine-tuning. Patterns are deterministic, auditable, and stored in git.

Act Safely

Start in observation mode. Graduate to filing issues. Then creating PRs. Then auto-merging. You control the autonomy level at every step.

agent-neo
flagship
"You don't need to think about air, it's just there, keeping everything alive... just like Neo."

Autonomous AI SRE that learns your infrastructure and fixes it. One Go binary, no Python. Drop a YAML manifest describing your environment — VCS, alerts, metrics, deploy pipelines — and Neo builds the integrations, observes signals, investigates anomalies, creates fixes, and learns from every resolution.

3-Tier Engine

Fast triage, focused investigation, full agentic code review. Progressive escalation.

Self-Learning

Signal diffing, pattern engine, semantic dedup. 1,400 signals per sweep, sub-second.

Scale

Workstation to 2,000 workers. SQLite to MySQL. Ollama to Claude. Your call.

Origin

open-ai.org was registered long before the other "OpenAI" existed. It stood for Open Source Automated Intelligence — born from years of writing Puppet code and automating everything that could be automated.

The premise was simple: infrastructure should manage itself. Configuration management DSLs were the first wave. Agent Neo is the next — same philosophy, but instead of declaring desired state in a DSL, you describe your environment in YAML and let an AI agent observe, investigate, and converge it.

The domain lived, the org evolved. The mission never changed: build open source tools that automate the intelligence layer of operations.
